# HouseOS Vision

HouseOS is the permanent timeline of a home.

The product helps people keep a clear, searchable history of what happens to the places they live in, own, rent, or manage. It is not primarily a task manager, appliance database, file cabinet, or smart-home dashboard. Those capabilities may become useful later, but the first product promise is simpler:

> Keep a timeline of everything that happens to your home.

## Product Promise

HouseOS helps users answer questions like:

- When did we replace the water heater?
- Where is the warranty for the dishwasher?
- Who repaired the garage door?
- What did the living room look like when we moved in?
- What happened when the basement flooded?
- What receipts, photos, and documents belong to this event?

Every interaction should leave the home better documented than it was before.

## Audience

The initial product is for individual homeowners and renters who want a better memory for their home. The architecture should also support future use cases such as families, landlords, property managers, adult children helping parents, and people tracking multiple homes.

## Positioning

HouseOS v1 is a memory and records app for homes.

It should feel useful enough for maintenance records, but warm enough for personal history. A house-level entry may be practical, like water damage, or emotional, like move-in day or Christmas morning in the living room.

## What Makes It Different

HouseOS is timeline-first.

Many home apps start with checklists, reminders, inventory, or maintenance schedules. HouseOS starts with the history of the home and lets items, files, search, and future reminders orbit that timeline.

## Long-Term Direction

As the record grows, HouseOS can become more powerful:

- robust search across documents, receipts, photos, and PDFs
- OCR and automatic document classification
- appliance label scanning
- maintenance suggestions
- reminders
- collaboration
- utility and renovation cost tracking
- home value and project ROI context
- AI-assisted retrieval and summarization

Those features should build on the timeline, not replace it.
